My Work Projects and Challenges

Testing at Mindmore

At Mindmore, I worked remotely as part of a team developing health-tech software.

Challenge: Mindmore is located at Stockholm, and I live in Lund. Remote work sometimes made communication and teamwork more difficult. The product was also related to health, which meant that regulations and data protection were very important.

What I learned: I learned the importance of clear communication when working remotely and became more aware of the extra care needed when testing software that handles sensitive information.

Testing at Relax Gaming

At Relax Gaming, I work with testing games in a highly regulated industry.

Challenge: There are many rules that games need to follow, and the games are becoming more complex everyday. This means that changes can affect different parts of a game and create new problems.

What I did: I improved test cases and adapted the testing process when requirements or game features changed.

What I learned: I learned to be more flexible and to think about how one change can affect several parts of a product.
